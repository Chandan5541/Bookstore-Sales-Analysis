# Bookstore Sales Analysis (SQL)

SQL analysis of a bookstore database to answer business questions about books, customers, orders and revenue.

## Business questions answered
- Books by genre, average price and most expensive books
- Customers with at least 2 orders
- Most frequently ordered book
- Total quantity of books sold by author and by genre
- Cities where customers spent over $30
- Customer who spent the most on orders
- Orders in a date range (August 2023)
- Total stock and total revenue generated

## SQL concepts used
SELECT & WHERE, ORDER BY (ASC & DESC), BETWEEN, DISTINCT, aggregate functions (SUM, AVG, COUNT), GROUP BY & HAVING, INNER JOIN, LIMIT

## Tools
PostgreSQL
