# 📚 Bookstore Sales Analysis | SQL

📄 [View all 19 queries and answers (PDF)](SECOND%20SQL%20PROJECT%20IN%20MS%20WORD.pdf)

SQL analysis of a bookstore database to answer 19 business questions about books, customers, orders and revenue.

## 📌 Business Problem
A bookstore wants to know which genres, authors and books sell best, who its most valuable customers are, and how much revenue and stock it has, so it can plan inventory and promotions.

## ❓ Business Questions Answered
| Theme | Business Question | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Books and pricing | Books by genre, average price and most expensive books | Guides pricing and catalog decisions |
| Customer loyalty | Customers with at least 2 orders | Identifies repeat buyers |
| Best sellers | Most frequently ordered book | Shows what to stock more of |
| Sales performance | Total quantity sold by author and by genre | Shows which authors and genres drive demand |
| Geography | Cities where customers spent over $30 | Finds high-value markets |
| Top customer | Customer who spent the most on orders | Targets VIP customers for offers |
| Time analysis | Orders in a date range (August 2023) | Tracks monthly sales activity |
| Business totals | Total stock and total revenue generated | Gives an inventory and revenue snapshot |

## 🔍 Key Insights
1. [Most expensive book and its price]
2. [Best-selling book and the quantity sold]
3. [Top genre by quantity sold]
4. [Top customer and their total spend]
5. [Total revenue and total stock]
6. [Number of orders in August 2023]

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Stock more copies of the best-selling books and genres.
- Offer loyalty rewards to customers with repeat orders.
- Run targeted promotions in cities with high customer spend.
- Reach out to the top-spending customer with exclusive offers.
